Privacy Policy

Last updated May 29, 2020

Please read this privacy notice carefully as it will help you make informed decisions about sharing your personal information with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information regarding your use of our website and our apps, including information you provide directly (such as your name and email address when using our contact form).

How We Use Your Information

We may process your data on the following legal bases:

Legitimate Interests: We may process your data when it is reasonably necessary to achieve our legitimate business interests.

Performance of a Contract: Where we have entered into a contract with you, we may process your personal information to fulfill the terms of our contract.

Legal Obligations: We may process your information where required by applicable law.

Vital Interests: We may process your information where necessary to protect your vital interests or those of a third party.

How We Share Your Information

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations.

Business Transfers: We may share or transfer your information in connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy notice unless otherwise required by law.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 18 years of age.

Your Rights

You may review, change, or terminate your account at any time. If you are a resident of California, you are granted specific rights regarding access to your personal information.

Policy Updates

We will update this policy as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. The updated version will be indicated by a revised date at the top of this page.

Contact Us
